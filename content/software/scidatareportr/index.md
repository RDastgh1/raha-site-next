SciDataReportR is positioned as reproducible reporting infrastructure: a system for turning complex scientific analyses into consistent, inspectable research outputs.

## Capability unlocked

- Automated report generation for cognitive and translational research.
- Reusable workflows for summaries, visualizations, and reproducible documentation.
- A foundation for transparent scientific communication across collaborators.
