SciDataAgent is an active development effort in responsible biomedical AI. The goal is not to replace scientists, but to help scientists think more effectively: asking better questions, exploring data conversationally, documenting assumptions, and connecting analytical choices to reproducible workflows.

Within the SciDataReportR ecosystem, SciDataAgent is positioned as a human-in-the-loop AI reasoning layer above open-source scientific software infrastructure. It is designed around transparency, reproducibility, scientific judgment, and rigorous interpretation.
