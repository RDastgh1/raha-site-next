MultiPlexReportR is part of the SciDataReportR ecosystem. It focuses on multiplex biomarker panels where biomedical teams need a reproducible path from assay QC to statistical analysis, visualization, and scientific interpretation.

The package emphasizes human-in-the-loop scientific reasoning: software can organize and surface evidence, but scientists remain responsible for interpretation, biological plausibility, and translational judgment.
